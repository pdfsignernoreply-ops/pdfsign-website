Compress PDF — FAQ

Is it safe and private?

Yes — PDFSign compresses entirely on your Windows PC. Your file is never uploaded, so confidential and government documents never leave your machine.

Can I compress a PDF to a specific size?

Yes. “Fit a size limit” lets you enter a target such as 5 MB, and PDFSign automatically finds the best quality that fits — ideal for portal upload limits.

Will the compressed PDF be accepted by GeM?

Yes. PDFSign writes a GeM-safe PDF (classic cross-reference table, no object streams), which government portals accept without a repair step.

Does it work on scanned PDFs?

Yes — scanned/image-heavy PDFs are exactly where compression helps most. You can also make them searchable with the built-in OCR tool.
