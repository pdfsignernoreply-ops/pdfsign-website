## 2. LONG DESCRIPTION (up to 1,500 chars — shown on product page)

PDFSign is a Windows desktop application for digitally signing PDF documents using USB DSC tokens or signature stamp images — entirely offline, with no Java, no browser plugins, and no cloud uploads.

**Who it's for:**
- CA and accounting firms signing large volumes of audit reports, balance sheets, and tax certificates
- Businesses submitting tenders on GeM, CPPP, NIC eProcurement, and other government portals
- Legal, finance, and compliance professionals handling sensitive documents
- Anyone who needs a fast, private, legally valid PDF signature

**Key capabilities:**
- Supports all major USB DSC tokens: HYP2003, ePass 2003/3003, ProxyKey (Watchdata), Feitian, and any PKCS#11-compatible token
- Batch sign hundreds of PDFs in one operation — no repeat clicks, no manual intervention
- Place your signature exactly where you want it by clicking on the PDF preview
- Embed RFC 3161 trusted timestamps so signatures remain legally valid after certificate expiry
- Restrict PDF printing, editing, or copying with built-in document security
- PAdES-compliant output accepted on all major Indian government portals
- Signature stamp image mode — add PNG/JPG signature images to any PDF without a DSC token

**Why professionals prefer PDFSign:**
- 100% offline — documents never touch a server
- Works with every CCA-licensed CA in India (eMudhra, Capricorn, NSDL, (n)Code, Pentasign, vsign, Sify)
- Under 60 seconds from open to signed PDF
- 30-day free trial, no credit card required
