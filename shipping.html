Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Digital Product — No Physical Shipment

PDFSign is a downloadable software product. It is delivered entirely online, so no physical goods are shipped and no shipping charges apply.

2. Delivery of Your Licence

Immediately after a successful payment, your licence key is generated and sent automatically to the email address provided at checkout — usually within a few minutes. You can download and install the PDFSign application at any time from pdfsign.in.

Delivery is electronic and instant. There is no waiting period, courier, or tracking involved.

3. If You Haven't Received Your Key

If your licence key does not arrive within a few minutes:

  • Check your Spam / Promotions / Junk folder.
  • Confirm the email address entered at checkout was correct.
  • Contact us and we will resend it promptly — see the details below.

4. Exchanges

Because PDFSign is a digital licence delivered electronically, licences cannot be physically exchanged. If you have an issue with your purchase or need help selecting the right plan, please contact our support team; any eligible request is handled under our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

5. Contact Us

For any question about delivery of your licence:

  • Email: help@pdfsign.in
