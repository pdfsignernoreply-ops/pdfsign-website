How to bulk sign multiple PDFs

Four steps to sign an entire folder — works with a signature stamp image or a DSC token.

1

Switch to Bulk Sign (Folder) mode and choose your signing mode — Signature Stamp Image or Digital Signature (DSC).

2

Pick a source folder of PDFs and a destination folder for the signed copies. Your originals are never modified.

3

Set your signature once — position, page (First / Last / All Pages), and stamp size or certificate. It applies to every file.

4

Click Sign. A progress bar runs through the whole folder. 100+ PDFs are signed in about a minute.
