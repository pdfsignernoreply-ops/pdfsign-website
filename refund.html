Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Free Evaluation

PDFSign offers a free evaluation — up to 30 days or 100 signed PDFs (whichever comes first) — so that you can fully evaluate the software before purchasing a licence. During the evaluation period, you can verify compatibility with your operating system, USB token, digital certificate, and workflow.

We encourage all users to use the evaluation to ensure the software meets their requirements before making a purchase.

2. Cancellation

PDFSign licences are one-time purchases for the plan you choose (Monthly, Annual, or Lifetime). They are not auto-renewing subscriptions — no recurring or automatic charge is ever made to your card or account, so there is no subscription to cancel to stop future billing. A Monthly or Annual licence simply stops working at the end of its paid term unless you choose to purchase again; you may stop using the software at any time.

Because your licence key is delivered to your email automatically and immediately after a successful payment, an order cannot be cancelled once payment has been completed. For this reason we strongly encourage using the free evaluation to confirm the software suits your needs before you buy. Any request made after purchase is handled under the Refund section below.

3. No Refunds After Purchase

Because PDFSign provides a fully functional free evaluation before purchase, all licence purchases are final and non-refundable.

Once a licence key has been issued and activated, we do not offer refunds for:

  • Change of mind
  • Accidental purchases
  • Incompatibility discovered after the evaluation period
  • Failure to use the software
  • Partial use of Monthly or Annual licences
  • Business requirement changes

This policy applies to all licence plans offered by PDFSign.

4. Exceptional Circumstances

Refunds may only be provided where they are required by applicable consumer protection laws.

5. Technical Support

If you experience any technical issue with PDFSign, our support team will make every reasonable effort to resolve the problem.

This includes assistance with:

  • Licence activation
  • USB Token configuration
  • PKCS#11 setup
  • Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) detection
  • Installation and activation issues
  • Software troubleshooting

Our goal is to ensure the software functions correctly before concluding that a refund is appropriate under applicable law.

6. Licence Reactivation

If you replace or format your computer, please contact our support team. We can assist with licence reactivation in accordance with our licence terms.
